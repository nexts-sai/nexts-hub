# FilmCraft Story and Script Editing

Adapted from the Restructure and Target-script sections of ChatCut's `other-a-roll-editing-scenarios.md`; see [provenance](../../references/chatcut-adaptation/README.md). Follow the [shared contract](../../references/workflow-contract.md) and [talking-head timing rules](../filmcraft-talking-head/SKILL.md).

1. Resolve the requested structure: chronological, topic-based, question/answer, conclusion-first or tutorial steps. If major restructuring has no target direction, ask that single material question before edits. A supplied script already establishes direction.
2. Read all relevant spoken content and select complete sentences, answers, ideas or steps. Map each desired unit to its exact source and current transcript address. Record unmatched script content; do not synthesize speech or stitch unfinished takes to pretend the source contains it.
3. For target-script editing, prioritize target meaning and order. Natural spoken paraphrases may match; choose the most complete, natural matching take. Exclude unrelated segment heads/tails and long surrounding context unless necessary for comprehension. Preserve names, numbers, negation and qualifications.
4. Save a recoverable version and discover FilmCraft's transcript-aware rearrangement capability. ChatCut's Script files and APIs do not exist here by assumption. Apply verified native operations only; a missing reorder/mapping capability blocks that change rather than justifying guessed frame placement.
5. Move complete semantic units. After each structural group, reread the new transcript and linked A/V state, refresh addresses, and check referents and connectors such as so, but, next and this. Do not split a sentence across unrelated positions or imply a different answer.
6. Compare the entire result against the target: no omitted requested content, accidental duplication, extra source or wrong order. Listen across each relocated seam and check continuity. Finalize timing before requested [captions](../filmcraft-captions/SKILL.md), [B-roll](../filmcraft-broll/SKILL.md), [audio](../filmcraft-audio/SKILL.md) or [graphics](../filmcraft-motion/SKILL.md).

For multiple independent versions or highlights, compose with [shorts](../filmcraft-shorts/SKILL.md). Do not shorten or crop a narrative restructuring request merely because that supporting skill also handles shorts.
