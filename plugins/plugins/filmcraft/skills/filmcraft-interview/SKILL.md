# FilmCraft interview

Use for interviews, documentary conversations, testimonials, and multi-speaker dialogue. Read [editorial techniques](../../references/editing-techniques.md) and [FilmCraft editing](../filmcraft-edit/SKILL.md).

## Editorial plan

- Inspect all takes and speakers before selecting. Build a transcript-backed paper edit: question, answer, supporting evidence, turn, and ending.
- Preserve the speaker's meaning and uncertainty. Remove repetition and dead air without changing claims or implying a different answer.
- Use J-cuts and L-cuts, room tone, two-shots, reaction shots, and restrained reframes to hide edits. Use B-roll to show evidence or place, not to cover every cut.
- Identify speakers with consistent lower thirds. Keep captions verbatim or clearly mark a translated/edited version; do not fabricate missing words.

## Execution

1. Create a transcript and select ranges for the story arc before mutating the project.
2. Assemble a dialogue-first paper edit and check continuity of speaker, screen direction, and chronology.
3. Add contextual B-roll, room tone, music, lower thirds, captions, and minimal emphasis cards in separate passes.
4. Discover FilmCraft MCP commands before each mutation and inspect clip, track, caption, and object IDs after each pass.
5. Review factual meaning, speaker identity, audio noise, caption timing, and the final narrative landing before export.

Do not cut an answer into a claim the speaker did not make, or use a reaction shot to imply an unspoken response.
