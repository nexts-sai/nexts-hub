# FilmCraft product short

Use for 10-45 second product clips, vertical social videos, and ecommerce demonstrations. Read [editorial techniques](../../references/editing-techniques.md), [FilmCraft editing](../filmcraft-edit/SKILL.md), and [video modes](../../references/modes.md).

## Editorial plan

- Default to 9:16 only when no delivery format is given. Confirm duration, platform, audience, product, and CTA before choosing a structure.
- Use hook -> problem or desire -> product reveal -> one or two benefits -> proof or result -> CTA. Keep one idea per shot and make the product readable in the first seconds.
- Maintain product geometry, material, color, and screen state across generated and supplied shots. Use match cuts, macro details, quick punch-ins, and action-led B-roll instead of random effects.
- Use large, high-contrast captions and cards inside the platform safe area. Keep music and effects below narration.

## Execution

1. Create a timed storyboard with shot purpose, visual, narration, caption, and CTA.
2. Assemble the shortest coherent rough cut before adding motion or transitions.
3. Add product demonstration, captions, sound accents, logo, and CTA as reviewable passes.
4. Discover FilmCraft MCP commands before each mutation and inspect the sequence after each pass.
5. Render a vertical preview, check hook clarity, product continuity, text cropping, audio, and exact duration before export.

Do not stretch a short source with black frames or invent product features to fill time.
