# FilmCraft promo

Use for brand films, campaign spots, launch videos, and service promotions. Read [editorial techniques](../../references/editing-techniques.md) and [FilmCraft editing](../filmcraft-edit/SKILL.md).

## Editorial plan

- Start with a visual or verbal hook that makes the audience understand the subject quickly.
- Structure the cut as context or problem -> product or service -> proof or benefit -> brand memory -> CTA. Change the order when the brief requires a different sales argument.
- Show the product in use, not only as a hero shot. Use macro details, screen recordings, demonstrations, before/after comparisons, and social proof only when supplied or verified.
- Keep brand typography, logo timing, color, music, and voice consistent. Use punch-ins, kinetic text, and speed changes to emphasize a benefit, never to hide missing proof.

## Execution

1. Extract the approved claims, audience, offer, duration, aspect ratio, and CTA from the brief.
2. Build a storyboard and a proof-first rough cut.
3. Add product B-roll, content cards, lower thirds, logo, music, narration, captions, and restrained motion graphics in passes.
4. Discover FilmCraft MCP commands before each mutation and inspect the timeline after each pass.
5. Review claim accuracy, logo and CTA legibility, audio mix, caption safe areas, and the first/last frame before export.

Never invent a product claim, testimonial, price, logo asset, or unsupported FilmCraft effect.
