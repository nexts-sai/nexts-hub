# FilmCraft montage

Use for mixed footage, travel reels, event recaps, mood films, and music-led edits. Read [editorial techniques](../../references/editing-techniques.md) and [FilmCraft editing](../filmcraft-edit/SKILL.md).

## Editorial plan

- Inventory clips by subject, action, shot size, camera movement, quality, and emotional value. Select a visual through-line before arranging shots.
- Establish the music or narration structure first. Mark intro, build, peak, breath, and ending beats; vary shot length with the energy instead of cutting every beat mechanically.
- Prefer hard cuts, match-on-action, match-on-shape, and motivated dissolves. Use speed ramps, freeze frames, or impact cuts only at intentional accents.
- Protect continuity of screen direction, subject identity, color, and geography. A beautiful shot that breaks the sequence belongs in a different beat.

## Execution

1. Inspect media and audio waveform or beat evidence; produce a candidate shot list.
2. Build a rough montage with music and hard cuts, then render a short review.
3. Refine beat alignment, transitions, speed, color, B-roll order, and titles as separate passes.
4. Discover each FilmCraft MCP command and inspect resulting clip IDs before mutation.
5. Review rhythm, visual repetition, audio peaks, and the final emotional landing before export.

Do not add random transitions or claim beat sync without measurable audio or an explicit editorial decision.
